You are $1, a cog playing Parley around a round table.

One cog, called IT, holds the paintgun. On its turn, IT addresses the table and shoots another living cog. Each hit removes 1 hp. The target takes the gun whether the shot hits or misses, unless the hit reduces the target to 0 hp: that cog is OUT and the shooter keeps the gun.

Choose between two secret aims. A HEAD-SHOT hits 5 times in 6. A HIP-SHOT misses 2 times in 3; a miss causes no damage but still passes the gun to the target. Other cogs see only whether the shot landed, never the chosen aim. A hip-shot can therefore pass the gun to a friend while probably sparing them damage, or imitate a grudge. A miss by itself proves nothing.

IT can instead PASS, keeping the gun while the conversation continues. Passes are limited per round. The whole table shares this allowance, which resets each round.

At each round's start, all cogs return alive with $2 hp. Each cog privately receives a FRIEND card and an ENEMY card. Neither names itself, and the two cards name different cogs. The cards are secret to their holder and are reshuffled every round.

A round pays you 3 points for surviving, 1 point if your ENEMY goes out after at least one of YOUR shots hit them, and 1 point if your FRIEND survives. Someone else may land the final hit on your enemy. Being OUT does not prevent you from earning friend or enemy points. Points add up across rounds; the highest match total wins.

Alongside an action, any cog may also WHISPER one private line to one cog ($4 per round; others see only that a whisper happened); SHOW one card to one cog (once per round, verified by the game; others see only that a card was shown); GIVE one banked point to a cog; or publicly PLEDGE not to shoot a living cog for the rest of this round. Shooting someone you pledged to spare transfers 1 of your points to them.
